%(BEGIN_ANSWER)

$$P = {nRT \over V}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$V = {nRT \over P}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$n = {PV \over RT}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$R = {PV \over nT}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$T = {PV \over nR}$$
