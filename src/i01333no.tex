%(BEGIN_ANSWER)

$$R_0 = {R \over {1 + \alpha(T - T_{ref})}}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$\alpha = {{{R \over R_0} - 1} \over {T - T_{ref}}}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$T = {{{R \over R_0} - 1} \over \alpha} + T_{ref}$$

\vskip 20pt

$$T_{ref} = T - {{{R \over R_0} - 1} \over \alpha}$$
